1. Terms and Conditions
This Agreement outlines the professional service structure between the Freelancer (Rosjos Ltd) and the Client. The objective is to ensure organized workflow management, timely delivery, confidentiality, transparent communication, and high-quality execution throughout the engagement. Both parties agree to operate in good faith and uphold the terms set herein from the effective date stated above.

3. Payment Terms
$100 retainer is due before commencement of any tasks. The remaining balance for the agreed scope is due one month in advance of the project start date.
Payment can be processed through Remitly or other avenues mutually agreed upon in writing by both parties before work begins.
Payment confirmation is issued within 24 hours of receipt. Work scheduling begins upon that confirmation.
Partial payments are not accepted unless explicitly agreed upon in writing prior to commencement.
Late or failed payment releases the reserved project slot. Rescheduling is subject to availability at that time.
The $100 retainer secures your slot on the schedule and is credited in full toward the total project cost.
Cancellation Policy
Cancellation 14+ days before start: full refund minus a $20 administrative fee.
Cancellation 7 to 14 days before start: 50% refund issued.
Cancellation under 7 days: no refund. The slot cannot be reallocated.
If the Freelancer fails to deliver: full refund processed within 5 business days.
4. Delivery & Quality of Work
Our Commitment to Exceptional Standards
All deliverables are crafted with precision, depth, and care. Every paper, task, and session reflects a genuine commitment to high-quality output tailored to your specific needs.
Academic papers are 100% original, thoroughly researched, and formatted to your requested citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.).
Deadlines are treated as commitments. All delivery timelines are confirmed in writing before work begins, and the client is notified immediately of any unforeseen delay.
One round of revisions is included at no additional cost for all written deliverables, ensuring the final product meets your expectations.
Tutoring sessions are structured, focused, and customized to each client's learning pace, subject area, and goals.
Quality concerns must be raised within 48 hours of delivery. Every legitimate concern is addressed promptly and professionally until full satisfaction is achieved.

This Agreement is the entire understanding between both parties. No verbal agreements supersede its written terms.
Client must provide all project requirements, materials, and instructions at the time of booking to avoid delays.
Modifications to this Agreement must be made in writing and mutually agreed upon by both parties.

Your privacy is non-negotiable. All work produced, communications exchanged, and personal details shared under this Agreement are held in strict, permanent confidence. Your identity, academic affiliation, and the nature of services requested remain entirely private. This confidentiality obligation is unconditional and survives the termination of this Agreement indefinitely.
